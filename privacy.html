Reel Poster – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Reel Poster is a private tool used only by the owner of the Badstrup Adventures channels (@badstrupadventures) to upload their own videos to their own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

YouTube API Services

Reel Poster uses YouTube API Services to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel. By using Reel Poster you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service (https://www.youtube.com/t/terms). How Google handles data is described in the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). Reel Poster uses the YouTube permission only to upload videos. It does not read, store or share YouTube data about the owner or anyone else, and it uses no cookies or tracking.

What it accesses

When the owner signs in, Reel Poster asks for permission to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel (and the equivalent permission on Instagram and TikTok: on TikTok the scopes user.info.basic, video.upload and video.publish). It uses this only to publish videos and captions that the owner has prepared and approved, and to read basic statistics about those videos.

What it stores

The sign-in tokens, the posting schedule and the video captions are stored only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sent to any server other than the platforms the videos are posted to (Google/YouTube, Meta/Instagram and TikTok).

Other people's data

Reel Poster does not collect, store, sell or share any information about other people, including viewers, followers or commenters.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com → Security → Third-party access (https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions), in Instagram under Settings → Apps and websites, and in TikTok under Settings → Security → Manage app permissions. When access is removed, the sign-in token stored on the owner's computer stops working. Deleting the tool's folder on the owner's computer removes all stored data.
